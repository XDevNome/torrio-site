Three ways in

Open it however suits you

Torrio Music comes with Torrio and needs no separate install. It runs on its own: the download manager doesn't have to be open.

From Torrio's icon

Right-click Torrio's icon next to the clock and choose Music player. The main window stays closed.

Its own desktop icon

Turn on its desktop or Start menu icon in Torrio › Options › Add-ons, and start it like any other app, with its own taskbar button.

Open with

Right-click a song or a playlist in File Explorer › Open with › Torrio Music. Your files keep their own default program.

Questions

Good to know

Is Torrio Music free?

Yes. It comes with Torrio, which is free, with no ads and no account.

Where does my library come from?

From the folders you choose, starting with your Music folder. Torrio Music reads the songs' details once and only looks at new or changed files afterwards.

Does closing the window stop the music?

No. The music keeps playing from Torrio's icon in the tray, where you can pause or skip; the keyboard's media keys work too.

Can I use my existing playlists?

Yes. Import M3U, M3U8 and PLS playlists, and export any playlist as M3U.

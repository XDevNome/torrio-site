How it works

Three steps, then it runs by itself

Sign in with Google

Open Google Drive in Torrio's sidebar and press Sign in with Google. Google's own sign-in page opens in your browser, and you choose what to allow there.

Tick what you want

Browse My Drive or Shared with me, or paste a Google Drive link someone shared with you. Tick files and whole folders.

Download

Everything you ticked becomes one download in Torrio's list. Its details show every file and how far each one has got.

Same folder structure

Folders arrive with everything inside them, sub-folders and empty folders included, exactly as they are on Google Drive.

Several files at once

Torrio downloads a few files at the same time, each over several connections, and can pause and resume.

Docs, Sheets and Slides

Google's own files are saved as Word, Excel and PowerPoint files (.docx, .xlsx, .pptx), converted by Google as they download.

One .zip if you like

Tick Save as one .zip and everything is packed into a single .zip on your PC once it has all arrived.

Shared links

Paste a link to a folder or file that someone shared with you, and download it the same way.

Read-only

Torrio can list and download your files. It can't change, move, share or delete anything on your Google Drive.

Your Google data

What Torrio accesses, and why

The full details are in our Privacy Policy.

What Torrio asks Google for

See and download all your Google Drive files (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.readonly). This is read-only access. Torrio needs it because you can pick any file or folder, including whole folders, items shared with you and shared links, and Torrio has to list what's inside them to download them.

What Torrio reads, and what it does with it

  • File and folder names, sizes, types and dates: to show your Drive in Torrio so you can choose what to download.
  • The contents of the files you choose: downloaded to the folder you pick on your PC. Nothing is downloaded unless you choose it.
  • Your Google account name and email address: shown in Torrio so you can see which account is signed in.

Where it's kept

  • Everything stays on your PC. Torrio has no servers for this, and your files and data are never sent to DevNome or anyone else.
  • Your sign-in is stored on your PC, encrypted by Windows for your Windows account only.
  • Google user data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never used to train AI or machine-learning models.

Stopping access

Press Sign out in Torrio's Google Drive section to delete the saved sign-in from your PC. You can also remove Torrio's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Files you've already downloaded stay on your PC until you delete them.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Torrio's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Questions

Good to know

Can Torrio change or delete my Google Drive files?

No. Torrio asks Google for read-only access. It can list and download your files, and nothing else.

Do my files pass through Torrio's servers?

No. Torrio has no servers for this. Files are downloaded directly from Google to your PC.

Can Google zip my selection first?

Google doesn't offer zipping to apps. Torrio downloads the files several at a time and, if you tick Save as one .zip, packs them into one .zip on your PC when they've all arrived.

Are there files Torrio can't download?

Google Forms, Sites and Maps have no file to download, so they're skipped. Google converts Docs, Sheets and Slides of up to 10 MB; larger ones can be downloaded from the Google Drive website. Files whose owner turned off downloading can't be downloaded.

How do I stop Torrio's access?

Press Sign out in Torrio's Google Drive section, which deletes the saved sign-in from your PC. You can also remove Torrio at myaccount.google.com/permissions at any time.

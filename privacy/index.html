Privacy Policy

For the Torrio app and the Torrio Integration browser extension · Last updated 11 November 2026

In short: Torrio collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The browser extension only talks to the Torrio app on your own computer, and nothing it handles is sent to us or to anyone else.

The Torrio Integration extension

The extension connects your browser to the Torrio app installed on the same computer, using the browser's built-in native messaging. It has no server of its own and sends nothing over the internet.

When you download something, the extension passes these details only to the Torrio app on your computer, so it can download the file the same way your browser would:

  • the address (URL) of the file or video, and the page it came from;
  • the file name, size and type the website reports;
  • the cookies and browser identification (user agent) for that website, because many sites only allow a download when they're included.

The extension doesn't read or store your browsing history, doesn't collect form data, passwords or messages, and doesn't sell, share or transfer any data. Its settings (such as whether it's on, and where you placed the video button) are kept in the browser's own storage on your computer.

Why the extension asks for its permissions

PermissionWhat it's used for
DownloadsTo notice a download starting, hand it to Torrio, and hide the browser's own download bar while Torrio handles it.
Access to websitesTo recognise downloadable files and videos on the pages you visit and show the download button on videos.
Web requestsTo see the file type and size a website sends, so only real downloads are handed to Torrio.
CookiesTo pass the website's cookies to Torrio for that one download, so sites that need you signed in still work.
Native messagingTo talk to the Torrio app on your computer.
Context menusFor the right-click items "Download with Torrio" and "Download all links with Torrio".
Scripting and tabsTo collect a page's links when you choose "Download all links", and to know which tab a video is playing in.
StorageTo remember the extension's settings.

The Torrio app

The app works entirely on your computer. Your downloads, download history, settings, music library and playlists stay on your computer. Torrio connects to the internet only to:

  • download what you ask it to, from the websites you choose;
  • check torrio.devnome.com for a new version;
  • download the free tools it uses (yt-dlp, ffmpeg and the player engine) from their official public pages on GitHub, when you first need them or choose to get them.

Like any website, these servers see your computer's internet address when it connects. We don't collect or keep it.

This website

torrio.devnome.com uses no cookies, analytics or trackers. It's hosted on GitHub Pages. The "Support Torrio" panel loads Ko-fi only when you open it; Ko-fi's own privacy policy applies to payments made there.

Children

Torrio doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

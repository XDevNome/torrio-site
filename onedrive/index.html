How it works

Three steps, then it runs by itself

Sign in with Microsoft

Open OneDrive in Torrio's sidebar and press Sign in with Microsoft. Microsoft's own sign-in page opens in your browser, and you choose what to allow there.

Tick what you want

Browse your files, or paste a OneDrive or SharePoint link someone shared with you. Tick files and whole folders.

Download

Everything you ticked becomes one download in Torrio's list. Its details show every file and how far each one has got.

Same folder structure

Folders arrive with everything inside them, sub-folders and empty folders included, exactly as they are on OneDrive.

Several files at once

Torrio downloads a few files at the same time, each over several connections, and can pause and resume.

One .zip if you like

Tick Save as one .zip and everything is packed into a single .zip on your PC once it has all arrived.

Shared links

Paste a OneDrive or SharePoint link to a folder or file that someone shared with you, and download it the same way.

Personal, work and school

Works with personal Microsoft accounts and with work or school accounts, unless your organisation blocks apps from signing in.

Read-only

Torrio can list and download your files. It can't change, move, share or delete anything on your OneDrive.

Your Microsoft data

What Torrio accesses, and why

The full details are in our Privacy Policy.

What Torrio asks Microsoft for

  • Read all files you can access (Files.Read.All), read-only: to list and download your files, files shared with you and shared links.
  • Sign you in and read your profile (User.Read): to show which account is signed in.
  • Keep access (offline_access): so you don't have to sign in again every hour or every time you open Torrio.

What Torrio reads, and what it does with it

  • File and folder names, sizes and dates: to show your OneDrive in Torrio so you can choose what to download.
  • The contents of the files you choose: downloaded to the folder you pick on your PC. Nothing is downloaded unless you choose it.
  • Your account name and email address: shown in Torrio so you can see which account is signed in.

Where it's kept

  • Everything stays on your PC. Torrio has no servers for this, and your files and data are never sent to DevNome or anyone else.
  • Your sign-in is stored on your PC, encrypted by Windows for your Windows account only.
  • Your data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never used to train AI or machine-learning models.

Stopping access

Press Sign out in Torrio's OneDrive section to delete the saved sign-in from your PC. You can also remove Torrio's access at any time: for a personal account at account.live.com/consent/Manage, for a work or school account at myapps.microsoft.com. Files you've already downloaded stay on your PC until you delete them.

Questions

Good to know

Can Torrio change or delete my OneDrive files?

No. Torrio asks Microsoft for read-only access. It can list and download your files, and nothing else.

Do my files pass through Torrio's servers?

No. Torrio has no servers for this. Files are downloaded directly from Microsoft to your PC.

Can OneDrive zip my selection first?

OneDrive doesn't offer zipping to apps. Torrio downloads the files several at a time and, if you tick Save as one .zip, packs them into one .zip on your PC when they've all arrived.

Does it work with work and school accounts?

Yes, with personal Microsoft accounts and with work or school accounts, including SharePoint shared links, unless your organisation blocks apps from signing in.
